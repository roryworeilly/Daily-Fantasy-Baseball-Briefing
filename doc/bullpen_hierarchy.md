# MLB Bullpen Hierarchy — 2026 Fantasy Baseball
*Last updated: March 24, 2026*

**KEY:**
- **Closer**: Officially designated closer, or pitcher most likely to get the team's next save chance.
- **Co-closers**: Two pitchers sharing closing duties.
- **Closer-by-committee**: No clear single closer; multiple candidates.
- **Primary setup**: Next-most likely to take over if closer is unavailable; top holds-getter; may get save chances on closer's nights off.
- **Secondary setup**: Third-in-line to close; good candidate for holds.
- **Middle relief**: Other pertinent bullpen relievers.
- **Sleeper**: Reliever who could provide holds or eventually graduate to closer role.
- **%** = Ownership percentage in fantasy leagues.
